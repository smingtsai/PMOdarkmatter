### 报告摘要:

Leptogenesis is among the most elegant mechanisms to generate baryon asymmetry in our universe, which only requires the existence of heavy right-handed neutrinos with CP-violating decays that generate lepton asymmetry. These right-handed neutrinos can be non-perturbatively produced directly after inflation, from the oscillating inflaton background. In this talk, we will revisit this mechanism during preheating using the quantum Boltzmann equations. This framework allows us to study the production and decay of heavy right-handed neutrinos in a consistent manner. Due to their fermionic nature, heavy neutrino production can saturate after a few inflaton oscillations when their occupation numbers approach unity. Their decays can deplete their occupation numbers, allowing more particles to be produced and generating a larger lepton asymmetry. Compared with the stable case, we find that the final baryon asymmetry can be enhanced by two orders of magnitude.

### 报告人简介:

Dr. Wenbin Zhao is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the International Centre for Theoretical Physics (Asia-Pacific) and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. He received his BSc in Physics from Nankai University in 2019, then studied at the University of Bonn, earning an MSc in Physics in 2021 and a PhD in Physics in 2025 under Prof. Manuel Drees. His research focuses on beyond-Standard-Model particle phenomenology at the particle–cosmology interface, including inflation and reheating, dark matter production, baryon asymmetry, neutrino mass origin, and related gravitational-wave signals. Recent work uses modular symmetry to understand inflation and reheating and their connection to the Standard Model flavor puzzle, and studies quantum effects in preheating and reheating. He has presented at international conferences including SUSY, DESY Theory Workshop, and PLANCK.
